# Sprint 2 Retrospective
## What went well: 
- Team communication and meeting cadence were consistent, and most members participated in discussions and work sessions.
- We clarified core features (attendance system, work journal, class directory, CI/CD requirements) and aligned on responsibilities.
- Initial workflows were established, giving the team a clearer direction.
- Feedback from Powell and Sammed helped refine priorities and validate parts of our approach.
- Met with the TA (Nov 21st) to get input on Activity Chart in Class Directory,
- Met with Tutor for user study
- We integrated the Standuply Slack Bot to help with biweekly standups

## What didn’t go well:
- Some requirements were unclear early in the sprint, causing rework such as definitions for work journal privacy, CI/CD expectations, ADRs
- Coordination across backend, frontend, and integration teams still lacked cohesion, slowing implementation progress.
## What can be improved 
- Standardize issue assignment with clear ownership to ensure balanced workload and accountability
- Improve definition-of-done through ADRs, issue templates, and clearer acceptance criteria.
