Frequently Asked Questions

As a Team Lead, you're responsible for coordinating team meetings, tracking team tasks, managing group formation, and submitting weekly evaluations. You also facilitate communication between your team and the course instructor.

Click the "Go to Class Directory" button on this dashboard. You can view the course roster, instructor information, meeting schedule, and all relevant class resources in one place.

Click the "Create Ticket" button on this dashboard. Fill out the ticket form with details about your issue, question, or feedback. The course instructor and teaching assistants will review and respond within 24-48 hours.

Use the Teams Meetings and Tasks button in the navigation bar. You can schedule meetings, assign tasks to team members, set deadlines, and track completion status. Share the meeting link with your team.

Access the Weekly Evaluation section to set mood scores and add direct quotes from team members. Evaluations are submitted per week and help track team morale and progress. Submit by Friday for the current week.

Attendance codes are announced during class. Open the attendance drawer and enter the code provided. Your attendance record is automatically tracked. Submit attendance within the same class session.

The Work Journal lets you document team meetings, discussion notes, and progress updates. Use it to keep records of decisions made and action items assigned during meetings.

Use the Group Formation Form accessible from the navigation bar. You can add team members, assign roles, and organize sub-groups if needed. This information helps structure team collaboration.
