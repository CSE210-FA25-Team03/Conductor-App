Frequently Asked Questions

You can access all your assigned classes through the Class Directory. Click on any class to view the roster, schedule, and instructor contact information. Your tutoring assignments are listed under each class.

Click the "Create Ticket" button on this dashboard. Fill out the ticket form with details about your issue, question, or feedback. The course instructor and teaching assistants will review and respond to your ticket within 24-48 hours.

Student schedules are available in the Class Directory under each class. You can view when students are available for tutoring sessions and coordinate meeting times directly with them. Use the class calendar to schedule sessions.

You can log your tutoring hours in your profile. Go to Profile → Work Log and enter your tutoring sessions with dates, times, and students worked with. Submit your weekly hours by Friday of each week for payroll processing.

If a student misses a scheduled session, document it in your work log. Send a follow-up message through the class directory and notify the instructor if this is a recurring issue. Submit a support ticket if additional follow-up is needed.

Instructor and TA contact information is available in the Class Directory. You can find their email addresses, office hours, and availability. For urgent matters, use the Support Ticket system to escalate your request.
